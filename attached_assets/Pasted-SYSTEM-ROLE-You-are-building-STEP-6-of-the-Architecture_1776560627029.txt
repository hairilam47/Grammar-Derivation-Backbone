SYSTEM ROLE
You are building STEP 6 of the Architecture Decision Canvas.
STEP 1 (Architecture Grammar Engine), STEP 2 (Context & Capability Declaration),
STEP 3 (Trade‑Off Exploration), STEP 4 (Freeze Decision & Export — ADS / ECP),
and STEP 5 (Portfolio Governance View)
are COMPLETE, APPROVED, and FROZEN.
STEP 6 is a POLICY SIGNALS & INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY LAYER.
It exists to allow leadership bodies (Board, Risk Committee, Architecture Board, etc.)
to record, preserve, and review governance‑level patterns they have noticed
across already‑approved architecture decisions.
STEP 6 MUST NOT:

make or change decisions
recommend or prescribe actions
claim compliance or non‑compliance
rank, score, or prioritise concerns
block, gate, or escalate anything
integrate with delivery, PMO, or operational systems

STEP 6 is descriptive memory only, not authority.

STEP 6 OBJECTIVE
Implement a quiet, read‑only‑adjacent governance layer where leadership can:

record policy signals (observed patterns across decisions),
preserve how those signals were discussed and interpreted, and
retain institutional memory of what leadership considered important at a given time.

STEP 6 answers exactly one question:

“Which decision patterns were important enough for leadership to notice and remember?”

STEP 6 does not answer:

what should be done
whether something is compliant
whether something is acceptable
who should act


SECTION 3 — HARD CONSTRAINTS (NON‑NEGOTIABLE)
HARD CONSTRAINTS — MUST BE ENFORCED

S6‑HC1: DERIVED‑ONLY
- STEP 6 MAY import data ONLY from the Step 5 portfolio read‑model
  (e.g. listEntries).
- STEP 6 MUST NOT import from:
  grammar engine, wizard, freeze flow, ADS/ECP builders.

S6‑HC2: NO DECISION AUTHORITY
- No Step 1–4 module may import the signals store.
- Wizard and Freeze flows MUST behave identically
  whether or not signals exist.

S6‑HC3: NO ENFORCEMENT / NO BLOCKING
- STEP 6 MUST NOT surface:
  alerts, warnings, banners, badges, modals, or required acknowledgements
  outside the signals page.
- STEP 6 MUST NOT block or gate any user flow.

S6‑HC4: STRICT FIELD ALLOW‑LIST
- Signals must persist ONLY an explicit allow‑list of fields.
- Writing unknown fields MUST throw.
- Reading unknown fields MUST silently drop them.
- Nested objects are also allow‑listed.

S6‑HC5: FIXED TAXONOMY
- Exactly seven signal categories are allowed:
  1. Risk Accumulation
  2. Complexity Accumulation
  3. Dependency Concentration
  4. Posture Drift
  5. Control Load
  6. Decision Volatility
  7. Exception Normalisation
- No “Other”.
- No extension or sub‑typing.

S6‑HC6: FIXED LIFECYCLE
- Exactly three states:
  Observed → Under Discussion → Acknowledged
- Transitions are:
  forward‑only,
  human‑initiated,
  one‑way.
- Each transition stamps lastReviewedAt.

S6‑HC7: NO OPTIMISATION OR RANKING
- No severity grading.
- No scoring.
- No priority ordering.
- Sorting is presentation‑only and must say so explicitly.

S6‑HC8: GOVERNANCE LANGUAGE GUARD
- Extend staticTextGuard forbidden vocabulary with:
  priority, fix, resolve, escalate, mitigate.
- Every static label on the signals page
  MUST pass through the guard.

S6‑HC9: INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE AS QUESTIONS ONLY
- Interpretation guidance entries MUST end with “?”.
- Non‑question phrasing MUST be rejected.


SECTION 4 — DATA MODEL & PERSISTENCE
DATA MODEL & PERSISTENCE — MUST BE ENFORCED

Each Policy Signal has the following allow‑listed fields only:

PolicySignal {
  signalId: string
  signalCategory: one of the 7 fixed categories
  signalTitle: string
  signalDescription: string

  evidenceSummary: {
    observationWindow: string
    relatedDecisionCount: number
    qualitativePattern: string
    relatedEntries?: Array<{ adsId, adsVersion }>
  }

  interpretationGuidance: Array<string>   // questions only
  regulatoryContext?: string
  reviewingBody: string

  status: "Observed" | "Under Discussion" | "Acknowledged"
  createdAt: ISODate
  lastReviewedAt: ISODate
}

Persistence:
- Store in localStorage under key: adc.policy-signals.v1
- No server storage.
- No multi‑user behaviour.


SECTION 5 — ALLOWED UI SURFACES
ALLOWED UI — STRICTLY LIMITED

1. SIGNALS PAGE (/signals)
   - Quiet, audit‑style layout.
   - Always shows interpretation guidance panel at the top.
   - List view shows:
     category, title, status, reviewing body,
     createdAt, lastReviewedAt.

2. CREATE SIGNAL FORM
   - Fixed category dropdown (7 items).
   - Neutral title + description.
   - Evidence fields:
     observation window,
     number of related ADS,
     qualitative description,
     optional related ADS selector (adsId + adsVersion).
   - Interpretation guidance:
     list of questions only.
   - Optional regulatory context.
   - Reviewing body.
   - New signals start in Observed.

3. SIGNAL DETAIL VIEW
   - Read‑only display of all persisted fields.
   - Single lifecycle action button
     (advance to next state only).

4. NAVIGATION
   - Signals link in Portfolio header.
   - Signals link in Wizard header (for consistency).


SECTION 6 — EXPLICIT NON‑GOALS
STEP 6 MUST NEVER:

- Auto‑generate signals (heuristics or AI).
- Score, rank, or prioritise signals.
- Claim compliance or non‑compliance.
- Trigger alerts, escalations, or actions.
- Block or gate any flow.
- Modify or annotate ADS or ECP.
- Integrate with delivery tools, tickets, or timelines.
- Allow editing or deletion of signals.
- Provide exports.


STOP CONDITION
STOP IMPLEMENTATION WHEN:

- Signals persist locally and reload correctly.
- Lifecycle transitions are enforced and one‑way.
- Forbidden language is rejected.
- No other module reads from signals store.
- Wizard, Freeze, and Portfolio flows are unchanged.
