This task introduces a new downstream, read‑only view that renders baseline institutional exposure surfaces for one approved architectural decision.
This is Phase 1 (Foundation) of Decision Exposure.
Phase 2 (Exposure Narratives) MUST NOT be implemented in this task.
The Decision Exposure View is:

descriptive, not prescriptive

OBJECTIVE
Implement a baseline Decision Exposure View that answers exactly one question:

“Given this approved decision, what institutional and operational surfaces does it touch?”

Phase 1 shows what is exposed, not how severe it is, who owns it, or what should be done.

EXACT DERIVATION LOGIC (LOCKED & VERBATIM)
The following mappings MUST be implemented as written.
They are structural, not heuristic.

1️⃣ Governance Domains
Purpose: show which oversight domains are structurally engaged.
Mapping rules (additive):


Data Protection & Privacy

sensitivityLevel = High
OR inScopeCapabilityIds include data‑handling capabilities
OR ecpConstraintCategories include data protection constraints



Auditability & Record Integrity

inScopeCapabilityIds include Audit & Compliance
OR ecpConstraintCategories include audit / logging constraints



Identity & Access Oversight

inScopeCapabilityIds include External Access or Internal Admin
OR ecpConstraintCategories include access‑control constraints



Financial & Reporting Controls

inScopeCapabilityIds include Reporting & Analytics
OR organisationContext implies regulated reporting environment



Jurisdictional / Cross‑Border Considerations

organisationContext implies multi‑jurisdictional use
OR ecpConstraintCategories include residency / locality constraints



✅ Render labels only.

2️⃣ External Scrutiny Vectors
Purpose: show who may reasonably examine the decision.
Mapping rules:


External Audit Scrutiny Likely

Governance Domains include Auditability & Record Integrity



Regulatory Inquiry Plausible

Governance Domains include Data Protection & Privacy
AND organisationContext = Government or Regulated Enterprise



Third‑Party Assurance Reliance Increases

ecpConstraintCategories include compliance / assurance
OR expected lifespan is long‑lived



Contractual / Partner Review Exposure

inScopeCapabilityIds include External Access
OR organisationContext implies inter‑agency or partner use



✅ Content expresses possibility only, never prediction.
