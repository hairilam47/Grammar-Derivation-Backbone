SYSTEM ROLE
You are building STEP 7 of the Architecture Decision Canvas.
STEP 1 (Architecture Grammar Engine), STEP 2 (Context & Capability Declaration),
STEP 3 (Trade‑Off Exploration), STEP 4 (Freeze Decision & Export — ADS / ECP),
STEP 5 (Portfolio Governance View), and STEP 6 (Policy Signals & Institutional Memory)
are COMPLETE, APPROVED, and FROZEN.
STEP 7 is a READ‑ONLY REFLECTIVE GOVERNANCE VIEW.
It exists to help an institution observe how its decisions and governance attention evolve over time.
STEP 7 MUST NOT:

make or change decisions
recommend actions or interventions
encode standards or targets
imply urgency, necessity, or correctness
enforce compliance or optimisation
grant new authority to any role

STEP 7 reflects institutional cognition, not institutional intent.

STEP 7 OBJECTIVE
Implement a reflective, observational layer that allows leadership to see:

how often architectural decisions are revised,
how governance attention (Policy Signals) changes over time,
where attention is concentrated, diffused, or absent.

STEP 7 answers exactly one question:

“How does this institution’s decision‑making and governance attention change over time?”

STEP 7 does not answer:

what should be changed,
which decisions are wrong,
which concerns are most important,
what actions must follow.

SECTION 4 — ALLOWED OBSERVATIONS
ALLOWED OBSERVATIONS — DESCRIPTIVE ONLY

STEP 7 MAY observe and display:

1. Decision Lineage
   - Revisions of a single adsId over time
   - Temporal spacing of revisions
   - Count of versions per logical decision

2. Governance Attention Over Time
   - Policy Signals displayed by category across time
   - State progression (Observed → Under Discussion → Acknowledged)
   - No ranking, no urgency framing

3. Memory Density
   - Count of acknowledged signals
   - Distribution by category (descriptive only)

4. Silence Awareness (Optional)
   - Areas with repeated decisions but no signals
   - Displayed without interpretation or labels


SECTION 5 — ALLOWED VIEWS (ILLUSTRATIVE)
ALLOWED VIEWS — ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT EXHAUSTIVE

1. DECISION LINEAGE VIEW
   - Timeline or list showing how a decision evolved
   - No judgement on frequency or stability

2. GOVERNANCE ATTENTION TIMELINE
   - Neutral display of signals over time by category
   - No highlighting of peaks or “critical periods”

3. MEMORY OVERVIEW
   - Snapshot of institutional memory accumulation
   - Focus on what was acknowledged, not what was acted upon


SECTION 6 — EXPLICIT NON‑GOALS
STEP 7 MUST NEVER:

- Recommend decisions or changes
- Rank or score decisions or signals
- Define targets, norms, or expectations
- Highlight “problem areas” or “hot spots”
- Generate alerts or follow‑ups
- Act as a compliance or audit engine
- Justify mandated architectural changes

If a visualization could be used to mandate action,
it is OUT OF SCOPE by definition.


SECTION 7 — INTERPRETIVE SAFEGUARD
INTERPRETIVE SAFEGUARD — NON‑NEGOTIABLE

Before any Step 7 feature is accepted,
it must pass this test:

“Could this display be used to justify
a required architectural change?”

- If YES → Reject the feature
- If NO  → Feature remains governance‑safe
