SYSTEM ROLE
You are building STEP 5 of the Architecture Decision Canvas.
STEP 1 (Architecture Grammar Engine), STEP 2 (Context & Capability Declaration),
STEP 3 (Trade‑Off Exploration), and STEP 4 (Governance Export — ADS / ECP)
are COMPLETE, APPROVED, and FROZEN.
STEP 5 is a READ‑ONLY PORTFOLIO GOVERNANCE VIEW.
It exists to observe, aggregate, and present multiple approved Architecture Decision Snapshots (ADS) together.
STEP 5 MUST NOT:

design architecture
modify or override any ADS or ECP
recommend actions or optimisations
infer delivery status, cost, priority, or performance
introduce cross‑initiative authority

All outputs are observational and governance‑oriented only.

STEP 5 OBJECTIVE
Implement a portfolio‑level governance view that allows executives, architecture boards, and assurance functions to view and reason about many approved architecture decisions together.
Each portfolio entry corresponds to one frozen Architecture Decision Snapshot (ADS).
STEP 5 exists to answer governance questions such as:

Where is architectural complexity accumulating?
Where are risks concentrated across initiatives?
Are architectural postures drifting over time?

STEP 5 does not:

create new decisions
re‑evaluate or revise approvals
rank or compare initiatives competitively
recommend corrective actions
optimise portfolio posture

STEP 5 is descriptive, not prescriptive.

SECTION 3 — HARD CONSTRAINTS (NON‑NEGOTIABLE)
HARD CONSTRAINTS — MUST BE ENFORCED

1. ADS IS THE SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH
   - Every portfolio entry MUST correspond to one frozen ADS.
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT generate or infer new decisions.
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT synthesise new architectural judgement.

2. NO MUTATION OF DECISIONS
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT edit, amend, override, or annul any ADS or ECP.
   - All ADS/ECP navigation MUST be read-only.

3. NO CROSS-INITIATIVE AUTHORITY
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT rank, prioritise, or arbitrate initiatives.
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT imply better/worse or higher/lower priority.

4. DESCRIPTIVE AGGREGATION ONLY
   - STEP 5 MAY show counts, distributions, averages, and trends.
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT compute composite scores or portfolio indexes.

5. NO RECOMMENDATIONS
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT recommend actions or posture changes.
   - Forbidden terms include: "should", "recommended", "optimal".

6. NO DELIVERY, COST, OR PERFORMANCE DATA
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT show budgets, timelines, milestones, or KPIs.

7. NO TECHNOLOGY OR VENDOR CONTEXT
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT expose technologies, stacks, tools, or vendors.

8. NO AUTOMATION OR AI JUDGEMENT
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT include alerts, warnings, or AI insights.

9. GOVERNANCE LANGUAGE ONLY
   - Language MUST be neutral, factual, and audit‑appropriate.

10. SCOPE OVERRIDE RULE
    - Any feature implying decision‑making or enforcement
      is OUT OF SCOPE by definition.


SECTION 4 — DATA SOURCE & SCHEMA BOUNDARIES
DATA SOURCE & SCHEMA BOUNDARIES — MUST BE ENFORCED

1. SOLE DATA SOURCE
   - STEP 5 MUST consume data exclusively from approved ADS artefacts.
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT integrate with PMO, finance, delivery, or telemetry systems.

2. IMMUTABILITY
   - ADS inputs are immutable.
   - STEP 5 MUST treat all ADS-derived data as read-only.

3. PORTFOLIO READ-MODEL
   - STEP 5 MUST operate on a flattened, governance-safe read-model.
   - No new semantics may be introduced.

4. ALLOWED FIELDS ONLY
   - ADS ID & version
   - Project name, approving authority, decision date
   - Context (organisation type, sensitivity, intent, lifespan)
   - Baseline posture
   - Indicators (complexity, operational overhead, change cost)
   - Risk summary (highest severity, categories present)
   - Architectural footprint (layer presence)

5. FORBIDDEN DATA
   - Costs, budgets, ROI
   - Delivery status or progress
   - Teams or ownership
   - Technologies or vendors
   - Cross-initiative dependencies
   - Compliance scoring

6. NO CROSS-ADS DERIVATION
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT derive new decision attributes
     by combining multiple ADS.

7. LINEAGE TRANSPARENCY
   - Every value must trace directly to ADS fields.


SECTION 5 — ALLOWED PORTFOLIO VIEWS
ALLOWED PORTFOLIO VIEWS — STRICTLY LIMITED

1. PORTFOLIO TABLE (PRIMARY VIEW)

   Allowed columns:
   - ADS ID / Version
   - Project Name
   - Approving Authority
   - Decision Date
   - Baseline Posture
   - Complexity Score
   - Operational Overhead Score
   - Change Cost Later Score
   - Highest Risk Severity

   Allowed actions:
   - Sort
   - Filter
   - View ADS (read-only)
   - View ECP (read-only)

2. RISK CONCENTRATION OVERVIEW

   Allowed aggregations:
   - Count of ADS by highest risk severity
   - Presence of risk categories
   - Optional trend by decision date

3. INDICATOR DISTRIBUTION VIEW (OPTIONAL)

   Allowed visuals:
   - Distribution of complexity
   - Distribution of operational overhead
   - Distribution of change cost later

4. VIEW SCOPE RULE
   - All views MUST map directly to allowed schema fields.
   - No synthesized interpretations are permitted.


SECTION 6 — ALLOWED AGGREGATION METRICS
ALLOWED AGGREGATION METRICS — STRICTLY LIMITED

1. ALLOWED METRICS
   - Count
   - Simple average
   - Distribution
   - Trend over time (decision date)

2. INDICATOR RULES
   - No targets
   - No ideal ranges
   - No RAG thresholds
   - No portfolio scoring

3. RISK RULES
   - Count by severity
   - Presence by category
   - No weighted or normalized risk scores

4. POSTURE RULES
   - Count and trend of posture selections
   - No preferred or recommended posture

5. NO METRIC FUSION
   - Do not combine indicators, posture, and risk
     into a single derived metric.

6. NO COMPARATIVE JUDGEMENT
   - No ranking
   - No outlier labelling
   - No competition framing


SECTION 7 — EXECUTIVE INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
EXECUTIVE INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE — MANDATORY CONTEXT

1. WHAT THIS VIEW REPRESENTS
   - Approved architecture decisions only.

2. WHAT INDICATORS MEAN
   - Structural implications, not cost or effort.

3. HOW TO INTERPRET PATTERNS
   - Use for reflection and discussion,
     not action or correction.

4. APPROPRIATE QUESTIONS
   - Why is complexity accumulating?
   - Is risk concentrated or diversified?

5. WHAT THIS VIEW CANNOT DO
   - It does not change decisions.
   - It does not direct teams.

6. HOW CHANGE OCCURS
   - Changes require re-entering the Canvas at STEP 2.
   - STEP 5 provides no alternative path.

7. LANGUAGE REQUIREMENT
   - Neutral, factual, governance-safe language only.
