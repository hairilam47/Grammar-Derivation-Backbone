Phase 5 adds a procedural governance lens that indicates when reconsideration of a decision becomes procedurally legitimate — without recommending, triggering, or automating change.

PHASE 5 OBJECTIVE
Implement a Decision Re‑Entry Lens that answers exactly one question:

“Given what is now visible, is it procedurally legitimate to initiate a new decision?”

Phase 5 governs legitimacy of reconsideration, not correctness of outcomes.
Phase 5 must never decide what to change — only whether reconsideration is reasonably justified.

ABSOLUTE NON‑GOALS
Phase 5 MUST NEVER:

recommend revisiting or changing a decision
rank decisions by reconsideration priority
express urgency, risk, or failure
trigger workflows, tickets, or approvals
auto‑spawn a new decision
judge correctness or success
instruct users on next steps

If Phase 5 answers “what should we do now?”, it has failed.

HARD CONSTRAINTS (NON‑NEGOTIABLE)
PH5‑HC1: READ‑ONLY
- Phase 5 MUST NOT create, edit, advance, or delete any artefact.

PH5‑HC2: SINGLE‑DECISION SCOPE
- Phase 5 renders for ONE decision only.
- No cross‑decision aggregation, ranking, or comparison.

PH5‑HC3: DERIVED‑ONLY
- Inputs are limited to:
  - ADS / ECP metadata
  - Phase 1–4 outputs
  - decision age and lifecycle markers
- No runtime metrics, delivery data, incidents, or external feeds.

PH5‑HC4: NO PRESCRIPTION
- Phase 5 MUST NOT imply obligation, action, mitigation, or escalation.

PH5‑HC5: NO URGENCY / SEVERITY
- Forbidden terms include:
  urgent, critical, severe, failed, priority,
  overdue, risk level, must.

PH5‑HC6: HUMAN‑INITIATED ONLY
- Phase 5 MUST NOT initiate or automate decision re‑entry.

PH5‑HC7: STRICT REMOVABILITY
- Removing Phase 5 must leave Phases 1–4 unchanged byte‑for‑byte.

OUTPUT MODEL — RE‑ENTRY SIGNALS (NOT ACTIONS)
Phase 5 outputs neutral Re‑Entry Signals, not recommendations.
Characteristics

Informational only
No ranking or severity
No call‑to‑action
No automation

Example Signals (Illustrative Only)

“This decision is being interpreted under contexts beyond its original approval scope.”
“Contextual divergence has been observed since the decision was approved.”
“This decision remains aligned with its original usage assumptions.”

Signals must never instruct or imply change.

RE‑ENTRY LEGITIMACY CONDITIONS (DERIVATION LOGIC)
Phase 5 may emit a re‑entry signal when procedural legitimacy conditions are met, such as:

The decision is repeatedly viewed under non‑baseline Phase 4 lenses
Phase 4 lenses materially differ from original approval assumptions
Responsibility pressure appears in functions not dominant at approval
Decision lifespan materially exceeds initial expectations

These are conditions, not triggers.
The absence of such conditions must also be representable.
