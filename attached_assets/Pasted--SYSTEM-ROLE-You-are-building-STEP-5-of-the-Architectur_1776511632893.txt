
SYSTEM ROLE
You are building STEP 5 of the Architecture Decision Canvas.
STEP 1 (Architecture Grammar Engine), STEP 2 (Context & Capability Declaration), STEP 3 (Trade‑Off Exploration), and STEP 4 (Freeze Decision & Export — ADS / ECP) are COMPLETE, APPROVED, and FROZEN.
STEP 5 is a READ‑ONLY PORTFOLIO GOVERNANCE VIEW.
It exists to observe, aggregate, and present multiple approved Architecture Decision Snapshots (ADS) together.
STEP 5 MUST NOT:

design architecture
modify or override any ADS or ECP
recommend actions or optimisations
infer delivery status, cost, priority, or performance
introduce cross‑initiative authority
All outputs are observational and governance‑oriented only.


STEP 5 OBJECTIVE
Implement a portfolio‑level governance view that allows executives, architecture boards, and assurance functions to view and reason about many approved architecture decisions together.
Each portfolio entry corresponds to one frozen Architecture Decision Snapshot (ADS).
STEP 5 exists to answer governance questions such as:

Where is architectural complexity accumulating?
Where is risk concentrated across initiatives?
How are architectural postures distributed over time?
STEP 5 does not:

create new decisions
re‑evaluate or revise approvals
rank or compare initiatives competitively
recommend corrective actions
optimise portfolio posture
STEP 5 is descriptive, not prescriptive.


SECTION 3 — HARD CONSTRAINTS (NON‑NEGOTIABLE)
HARD CONSTRAINTS — MUST BE ENFORCED

1. ADS IS THE SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH
   - Every portfolio entry MUST correspond to one frozen ADS.
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT generate or infer new decisions.

2. NO MUTATION OF DECISIONS
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT edit, amend, override, or annul any ADS or ECP.
   - Navigation to ADS/ECP MUST be read‑only.

3. NO CROSS‑INITIATIVE AUTHORITY
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT prioritise, rank, or arbitrate initiatives.
   - Sorting is a PRESENTATION convenience only and MUST NOT imply desirability or priority.

4. DESCRIPTIVE AGGREGATION ONLY
   - Allowed: count, simple average, distribution.
   - Forbidden: composite scores, portfolio indexes, weighted metrics.

5. NO RECOMMENDATIONS
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT recommend actions or posture changes.
   - Forbidden words include: "should", "recommended", "optimal".

6. NO DELIVERY, COST, OR PERFORMANCE DATA
   - No budgets, timelines, KPIs, or execution signals.

7. NO TECHNOLOGY OR VENDOR CONTEXT
   - STEP 5 MUST NOT expose stacks, tools, products, or vendors.

8. NO AUTOMATION OR AI JUDGEMENT
   - No alerts, warnings, or AI‑generated insights.

9. GOVERNANCE LANGUAGE ONLY
   - Language MUST be neutral, factual, and audit‑appropriate.

10. SCOPE OVERRIDE RULE
    - Any feature implying decision authority is OUT OF SCOPE.




SECTION 4 — DATA SOURCES & SCHEMA BOUNDARIES
DATA SOURCE & SCHEMA BOUNDARIES — MUST BE ENFORCED

1. SOLE DATA SOURCE
   - STEP 5 MUST consume data exclusively from approved ADS artefacts.
   - No PMO, finance, delivery, or telemetry data permitted.

2. ADS IDENTITY VS VERSION
   - adsId represents the logical decision identity.
   - adsVersion represents the deterministic architectural version hash.
   - addOrUpdate rules:
     * same adsId + same adsVersion ⇒ update metadata only
     * same adsId + different adsVersion ⇒ new portfolio entry

3. PORTFOLIO READ‑MODEL ONLY
   - STEP 5 MUST operate on a flattened read‑model derived from ADS.
   - No new semantics may be introduced.

4. ALLOWED FIELDS ONLY
   - ADS ID, ADS version
   - Project name, approving authority, decision date
   - Decision context
   - Baseline posture
   - Indicator scores
   - Risk summary (highest severity + categories present)
   - Architectural footprint (layer presence)

5. FORBIDDEN DATA
   - Cost, budget, ROI
   - Delivery status or progress
   - Teams or ownership
   - Technologies or vendors
   - Cross‑initiative dependencies

6. LINEAGE TRANSPARENCY
   - Every portfolio value must trace directly to ADS fields.




SECTION 5 — ALLOWED PORTFOLIO VIEWS
ALLOWED PORTFOLIO VIEWS — STRICTLY LIMITED

1. PORTFOLIO TABLE (PRIMARY VIEW)
   Columns:
   - ADS ID / Version
   - Project Name
   - Approving Authority
   - Decision Date
   - Baseline Posture
   - Complexity Score
   - Operational Overhead Score
   - Change Cost Later Score
   - Highest Risk Severity

   Allowed actions:
   - Sort (display only)
   - Filter
   - View ADS (read‑only)
   - View ECP (read‑only)

2. RISK CONCENTRATION OVERVIEW
   - Count of ADS by highest risk severity
   - Distribution of risk categories

3. INDICATOR DISTRIBUTION
   - Distribution of complexity
   - Distribution of operational overhead
   - Distribution of change cost later




SECTION 6 — ALLOWED AGGREGATION METRICS
AGGREGATION METRICS — DESCRIPTIVE ONLY

Allowed:
- Count
- Simple average
- Distribution

Forbidden:
- Portfolio risk scores
- Weighted or normalised metrics
- Target or optimal ranges
- Metric fusion




SECTION 7 — EXECUTIVE INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
EXECUTIVE INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE — MUST BE VISIBLE

- This view shows approved architecture decisions, not delivery status.
- Indicators reflect structural implications, not cost or effort.
- Patterns prompt discussion, not action.
- Any change requires re‑entry at STEP 2 of the canvas.




SECTION 8 — EXPLICIT NON‑GOALS
STEP 5 MUST NEVER:
- Modify ADS or ECP
- Recommend actions
- Rank initiatives
- Introduce cost or delivery data
- Optimise portfolio posture
- Visualise technologies or vendors
- Act as a governance or approval engine
