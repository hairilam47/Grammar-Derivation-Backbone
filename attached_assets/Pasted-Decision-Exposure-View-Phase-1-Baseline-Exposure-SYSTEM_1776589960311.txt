Decision Exposure View — Phase 1 (Baseline Exposure)

SYSTEM ROLE
You are implementing Phase 1 of the Decision Exposure View in the Architecture Decision Canvas.
Steps 1–7 of the core system are COMPLETE and FROZEN.
This task introduces a new downstream, read‑only view that visualises baseline exposure surfaces for one approved architectural decision.
This is Phase 1 (Foundation) of Decision Exposure.
Phase 2 (Exposure Narratives) MUST NOT be implemented in this task.
The Decision Exposure View is:

descriptive, not prescriptive
observational, not evaluative
read‑only, not authoritative

It must never resemble a dashboard.

OBJECTIVE
Implement a read‑only Decision Exposure View that answers one question only:

“Given this approved decision, what institutional and operational surfaces does it touch?”

Phase 1 surfaces what is exposed, not how bad, who owns it, or what should be done.

NON‑GOALS (ABSOLUTE)
You MUST NOT:

recommend action
rank or score exposure
compare decisions
predict incidents or outcomes
assign ownership or accountability
persist new data
introduce charts, metrics, or heatmaps
duplicate or replace LeanIX‑style EA modelling

If a feature implies urgency or mandates action, it is out of scope.

ROUTING & SCOPE
Route: /exposure/:adsId


Renders one decision only
No aggregation across decisions
No list views
No global summaries

If the decision does not exist, show a neutral “Decision not found” message.

DATA SOURCES (READ‑ONLY)
Phase 1 MUST derive everything exclusively from:

ADS (Architecture Decision Snapshot)
ECP (Execution Constraint Profile)
Portfolio entry (for adsId / adsVersion resolution only)

❌ No runtime systems
❌ No delivery data
❌ No ownership metadata
❌ No new persistence

BASELINE EXPOSURE SECTIONS (MANDATORY)
Render exactly four sections, in this order:

Governance Domains
External Scrutiny Vectors
Impact Surfaces
Organisational Functions Affected

Each section renders:

a plain list of items (unordered)
no scores, no counts, no emphasis


EXACT DERIVATION LOGIC (LOCKED)
The following derivation rules MUST be encoded exactly.
They are structural mappings, not heuristic guesses.

1️⃣ Governance Domains
Purpose: Show which high‑level oversight domains are structurally engaged.
Input signals:

ADS.organisationContext
ADS.sensitivityLevel
ADS.capabilityScope
ECP constraint categories

Mapping rules (additive, non‑exclusive):


Data Protection & Privacy

sensitivityLevel = High
OR capability includes data‑handling (Case Management, Document Management)
OR ECP includes data protection constraints



Auditability & Record Integrity

capability includes Audit & Compliance
OR ECP includes immutable logging / audit constraints



Identity & Access Oversight

capability includes External Access OR Internal Admin
OR ECP includes access control constraints



Financial & Reporting Controls

capability includes Reporting & Analytics
OR organisationContext implies regulated reporting environment



Jurisdictional / Cross‑Border Considerations

organisationContext implies multi‑jurisdiction
OR ECP includes data residency / locality constraints



✅ Render domains as labels only, never explanations in Phase 1.

2️⃣ External Scrutiny Vectors
Purpose: Show who may reasonably examine this decision.
Input signals:

organisationContext
governance domains already derived
ADS risk category presence

Mapping rules:


External Audit Scrutiny Likely

Governance Domains include Auditability & Record Integrity



Regulatory Inquiry Plausible

Governance Domains include Data Protection & Privacy
AND organisationContext = Government or Regulated Enterprise



Third‑Party Assurance Reliance Increases

ECP includes compliance or assurance constraints
OR decision lifecycle = long‑lived system



Contractual / Partner Review Exposure

capability includes External Access
OR organisationContext implies inter‑agency or partner usage



✅ These are possibility surfaces, not predictions.

3️⃣ Impact Surfaces
Purpose: Show where sustained pressure accumulates if the decision is exercised.
Render three sub‑lists (no prioritisation):
Institutional
Derived when:

multiple governance domains exist
ECP category count > baseline

Labels may include:

governance coordination load
audit preparation effort
decision defensibility obligations

Product
Derived when:

capability scope includes external‑facing features
sensitivityLevel ≥ Medium

Labels may include:

feature constraint surface
user trust expectations
lifecycle rigidity

Infrastructure
Derived when:

ECP includes availability, resilience, recovery constraints
distributed or long‑lived system context

Labels may include:

operational resilience obligations
monitoring expectations
recovery preparedness

✅ No failure modes, no “will break”, no severity.

4️⃣ Organisational Functions Affected
Purpose: Show which parts of the institution will carry responsibility pressure.
Mapping rules:


Legal / Compliance

Data Protection or Regulatory governance domains present



Security Operations

Identity & Access Oversight present



Data Governance

Data Protection & Privacy present



Platform / Infrastructure

Infrastructure impact surface present



Customer Support

External Access capability present



Procurement / Vendor Management

ECP includes third‑party or external dependencies



✅ Alphabetical ordering only
✅ Not ownership, not accountability

UI REQUIREMENTS

All sections visible at once
Equal visual weight across sections
No directional arrows
No colour semantics (neutral tones only)
No icons that imply warning or importance

Above all sections, render a permanent interpretation banner:

“This view shows where an approved decision places institutional exposure.
It does not assess, rank, recommend, or require action.”


EMPTY STATES

If exposure cannot be derived for a section:

Render the section title
Show a neutral sentence:

“No exposure surfaces identified for this decision.”





No CTA. No implication of missing data.
